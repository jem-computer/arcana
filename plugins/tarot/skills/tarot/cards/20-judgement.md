# 20 - Judgement

## Traditional Divinatory Meanings

**Upright**: Awakening, calling, reckoning, renewal, forgiveness, absolution, inner calling, resurrection, self-evaluation, rebirth, higher purpose

**Reversed**: Self-doubt, refusal of calling, harsh self-judgment, inability to forgive, avoiding accountability, ignoring inner voice

**Keywords**: Awakening, calling, reckoning, renewal, forgiveness, absolution, resurrection, rebirth, purpose, evaluation

## Archetypal Psychology

**Core Archetype**: Awakening, the Call, the moment of reckoning and resurrection, hearing the trumpet of higher purpose

**Psychological Pattern**: The capacity for profound self-evaluation and response to higher calling. Represents the part of psyche that can step back and assess the entire journey, forgiving what needs forgiving and integrating all experience into wisdom. The moment when scattered fragments of self are called together and resurrected into new wholeness. Not the judgment of condemnation but the discernment that leads to rebirth. The inner voice calling to higher purpose.

**Shadow Aspects**: Harsh self-judgment without compassion, refusal to heed inner calling, using spirituality to avoid practical responsibility, inability to forgive self or others, waiting for external validation of inner knowing, resurrection without integration, rebirth without death

**Integration**: Self-evaluation balanced with self-compassion, hearing the call and responding with courage, forgiving while remaining accountable, integrating all aspects of journey into wisdom, resurrecting into wholeness, aligning with authentic purpose

## Narrative/Journey Context

**Position in Fool's Journey**: Twentieth card - after Sun's radiance, The Fool hears the call to higher purpose

**Story Arc**: The Fool, now radiant with integrated wisdom, hears the trumpet call to something higher. All that has been learned, suffered, celebrated, and transformed is now evaluated and integrated. The Fool is called to resurrection - not return to what was, but birth into what can be. This is the moment of reckoning where the entire journey is seen clearly and understood. The Fool must decide whether to answer the call to higher purpose.

**Relationship to Other Cards**:
- Follows The Sun: Radiant self → higher calling
- Precedes The World: Awakening → completion
- Contrasts Justice: Divine judgment/mercy ↔ karmic judgment/balance
- Mirrors Death: Both are profound transformations, Death ends while Judgement resurrects

**When This Archetype Appears**:
- Hearing inner calling to higher purpose
- Self-evaluation and life review
- Forgiveness and absolution
- Awakening to spiritual truth
- Resurrection and renewal
- Integration of all life experience
- Responding to divine or authentic calling
- Times when past must be reconciled for future to unfold
