# 10 - Wheel of Fortune

## Traditional Divinatory Meanings

**Upright**: Cycles, fate, turning points, karma, luck, change, destiny, life cycles, good fortune, unexpected events, pivotal moments

**Reversed**: Bad luck, resistance to change, breaking cycles, lack of control, unwanted changes, missed opportunities

**Keywords**: Cycles, fate, turning points, karma, luck, change, destiny, fortune, wheel, timing

## Archetypal Psychology

**Core Archetype**: Fate, the Wheel, the cyclical nature of existence, the turning of fortune

**Psychological Pattern**: Recognition that life moves in cycles beyond individual control. Represents the part of psyche that encounters forces larger than ego - karma, fate, synchronicity, the collective unconscious. The realization that "this too shall pass" applies to both fortune and misfortune. Understanding that being is a dynamic process rather than static state. The ego's relationship to forces it cannot control.

**Shadow Aspects**: Passive fatalism that denies agency ("it's just my fate"), addiction to drama and crisis at cycle turning points, refusal to accept change, believing only in luck rather than cause and effect, gambling mentality, chaos addiction, using "karma" to avoid responsibility

**Integration**: Accepting the reality of cycles while maintaining agency within them, understanding karma as pattern not punishment, flowing with change rather than resisting, recognizing synchronicity, balancing surrender to larger forces with personal responsibility

## Symbolic Correspondences

**Element**: Fire (transformation, energy, Jupiter's element of expansion)
**Astrological**: Jupiter (expansion, fortune, higher meaning, beneficence, cycles)
**Numerology**: 10 (completion of cycle, new beginning, 1+0=1 returns to unity)
**Colors**: Blue (wisdom, depth), gold (fortune), red (action, life force)
**Symbols**:
- Wheel (cycles, fortune, fate, eternal return)
- Four fixed signs (Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius - stability within change)
- Sphinx at top (riddle of existence, guardian of wisdom)
- Anubis ascending (rise of fortune, guide)
- Typhon descending (fall of fortune, chaos)
- TORA/ROTA/TARO (Torah, wheel, tarot - sacred teaching)
- Alchemical symbols (transformation, transmutation)

**Mythological**: The Moirai/Fates (spinners of destiny), Fortuna (goddess of luck), Ouroboros (eternal cycle), Buddhist wheel of dharma (teaching, karma)

## Narrative/Journey Context

**Position in Fool's Journey**: Tenth card - after finding inner wisdom, The Fool encounters forces beyond personal control

**Story Arc**: The Fool discovers that despite all the mastery and wisdom gained, life still turns on wheels beyond individual control. Encounters the reality of fate, karma, synchronicity, and the cyclical nature of existence. Learns to work with rather than against these larger forces. Understands that both good and bad fortune are temporary phases in endless cycles.

**Relationship to Other Cards**:
- Follows The Hermit: Inner wisdom → cosmic forces
- Precedes Justice: Fate → consequences and balance
- Contrasts The Magician: Individual will ↔ larger forces
- Mirrors Death: Both involve inevitable change and cycles

**When This Archetype Appears**:
- Major turning points and transitions
- Recognizing patterns and cycles
- Synchronicity and meaningful coincidence
- Accepting changes beyond control
- Understanding karma and consequences
- Timing and right action at pivotal moments
- Good or bad fortune arriving unexpectedly
- Completing one cycle and beginning another
